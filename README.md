## Section 1: Month-End Close & Financial Reporting

### Q1: Walk us through your experience managing a month-end close process from start to finish.

**What they're testing:** Do you understand the end-to-end [month-end close](https://www.netsuite.com/portal/resource/articles/accounting/month-end-close.shtml) cycle?

**Model Answer:**
"In my current/previous role, I managed the full month-end cycle including reviewing journals — [payroll journals](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/payroll.asp), credit cards, bank transactions, [revenue accruals](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/a/accrued-revenue.asp) and intercompany recharges. I ensured all [balance sheet reconciliations](https://www.highradius.com/resources/Blog/balance-sheet-reconciliation/) were completed accurately and on time. I'd review the [P&L](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/plstatement.asp) and [balance sheet](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/b/balancesheet.asp) in our ERP system, flag discrepancies with the finance team, and coordinate any adjustments needed. I also built monthly P&L and [ARR](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/a/arr.asp) reports for senior leadership. I maintained a close checklist with deadlines to ensure nothing was missed."

---

### Q2: How do you ensure accuracy and completeness of balance sheet reconciliations?

**What they're testing:** Attention to detail and understanding of [reconciliation processes](https://www.highradius.com/resources/Blog/balance-sheet-reconciliation/).

**Model Answer:**
"I follow a structured approach — each reconciliation has a standardised template with supporting documentation. I match balances to [sub-ledgers](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/s/subledger.asp), investigate aged or unusual items, and escalate anything unresolved. I set internal deadlines ahead of the official cut-off to allow time for review. I also cross-check with the P&L to catch anything that may have been misclassified. Where I've identified recurring issues, I've worked to automate or streamline the process to reduce manual error."

---

### Q3: Tell us about a time you identified an error during month-end. How did you handle it?

**What they're testing:** Problem-solving, initiative, and understanding of [journal entries](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/j/journal-entry.asp).

**Model Answer:**
"During a month-end review, I noticed a [revenue accrual](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/a/accrued-revenue.asp) was significantly higher than expected. I traced it back to a contract that had been renewed at a lower rate, but the accrual hadn't been updated. I raised it with the commercial finance team, corrected the journal, and then implemented a monthly reconciliation between the CRM contract values and the accruals schedule to prevent it happening again."

---

### Q4: What experience do you have with NetSuite and Anaplan?

**What they're testing:** ERP and planning tool familiarity.

**Model Answer (if experienced):**
"I have experience working with [NetSuite](https://www.netsuite.com/portal/products/erp/financial-management.shtml) for general ledger management, journal entries, balance sheet reconciliations and reporting. For [Anaplan](https://www.anaplan.com/blog/what-is-anaplan/), I've used it for budgeting, forecasting and P&L reporting. I'm comfortable navigating both systems and liaising with system teams when configuration changes are needed."

**Model Answer (if no direct experience):**
"While I haven't used NetSuite/Anaplan specifically, I have experience with [similar ERP systems] and I'm confident I can pick up new systems quickly. I've already started familiarising myself with NetSuite through their online resources. The underlying accounting principles are the same regardless of the platform."

## Section 2: Statutory Compliance & Tax

### Q5: What is your experience preparing financial statements for ASIC lodgement?

**What they're testing:** Understanding of Australian [statutory reporting obligations](https://asic.gov.au/regulatory-resources/financial-reporting-and-audit/).

**Model Answer:**
"I've been involved in preparing [general-purpose or special-purpose financial statements](https://www.cpaaustralia.com.au/tools-and-resources/financial-reporting) in accordance with [Australian Accounting Standards (AASB)](https://aasb.gov.au/pronouncements/accounting-standards/). This includes drafting the notes to the financial statements, ensuring compliance with the [Corporations Act 2001](https://www.legislation.gov.au/Details/C2024C00276), and coordinating with auditors for sign-off before lodgement with [ASIC](https://asic.gov.au/). I understand the key deadlines and reporting obligations for proprietary companies."

**Model Answer:**
"I've managed the full cycle of Australian tax compliance including:

### Q7: How do you stay current with changes to accounting standards?

**What they're testing:** Professional development mindset.

**Model Answer:**
"I subscribe to updates from the [AASB](https://aasb.gov.au/) and professional bodies like [CPA Australia](https://www.cpaaustralia.com.au/) or [CA ANZ](https://www.charteredaccountantsanz.com/). When a new standard is issued, I assess its impact on our financial reporting and implement required changes. For example, with revenue recognition changes under [AASB 15](https://www.iasplus.com/en/standards/aasb/aasb15), I worked through our contract portfolio to ensure we were recognising revenue appropriately."

## Section 3: Audit

### Q8: Describe your experience working with external auditors.

**What they're testing:** Ability to manage audit relationships and deliver under pressure.

**Model Answer:**
"I've been the primary point of contact for external auditors (such as [PwC](https://www.pwc.com.au/)) across multiple audit cycles. My approach is to prepare a comprehensive audit pack in advance — including [trial balance](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/t/trial_balance.asp), reconciliations, schedules, and supporting documents. I coordinate requests across finance, statutory, and tax teams, track outstanding items on a shared tracker, and ensure deadlines are met. Being proactive significantly reduces audit queries."

---

### Q9: How would you handle a disagreement with an auditor on an adjustment?

**What they're testing:** Professional judgement, communication skills.

**Model Answer:**
"I'd first make sure I fully understand their position and the accounting standard they're referencing. Then I'd present my reasoning with supporting evidence — contract terms, accounting policy, or precedent from prior periods. If we can't reach agreement, I'd escalate within both teams. The key is maintaining a professional, evidence-based discussion. If the adjustment is [immaterial](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/m/materiality.asp), I'd weigh the cost of the debate against the impact."

## Section 4: Budgeting & Forecasting

### Q10: How have you contributed to building budgets and forecasts?

**What they're testing:** Commercial awareness and stakeholder collaboration.

**Model Answer:**
"I've worked closely with country directors, sales teams, and account managers to build annual budgets and quarterly forecasts for both P&L and [ARR](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/a/arr.asp). My approach is to start with historical performance, overlay known changes (new deals, [churn](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/c/churnrate.asp), price uplifts), and stress-test assumptions with the commercial team. I prepare [QBR](https://www.salesforce.com/resources/articles/quarterly-business-review/) reports comparing actuals to budget and prior year, with [variance analysis](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/v/variance.asp) explaining key movements."

---

### Q11: How do you track ARR and what metrics do you report on?

**What they're testing:** SaaS finance knowledge.

**Model Answer:**
"I track [ARR](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/a/arr.asp) by breaking it into components — new business, upsells, renewals, downgrades, and [churn](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/c/churnrate.asp). I maintain a [waterfall/bridge analysis](https://www.wallstreetprep.com/knowledge/bridge-analysis/) showing the movement from opening to closing ARR each period. I also segment by entity, product line, or customer where needed. For QBR reporting, I include ARR trends, [renewal rates](https://www.klipfolio.com/resources/kpi-examples/saas/renewal-rate), and any at-risk contracts."

## Section 5: Collections & Credit Control

### Q12: Tell us about your experience in collections and managing overdue accounts.

**What they're testing:** Practical [accounts receivable](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/a/accountsreceivable.asp) management skills.

**Model Answer:**
"I've managed collections in collaboration with credit control teams, including contacting customers via phone and email for outstanding invoices. When accounts are overdue, I investigate the root cause — billing error, contract dispute, or late payment. I collaborate with sales, account managers, and internal teams to resolve issues. I reconcile accounts by reviewing contracts against invoices, and I maintain a weekly [ageing report](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/a/aging.asp) with insights for senior leadership."

---

### Q13: How do you handle a key customer who consistently pays late?

**What they're testing:** Stakeholder management and problem-solving.

**Model Answer:**
"I'd first investigate whether there's an underlying issue — incorrect billing details, [PO](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/purchase-order-lead-time.asp) requirements not being met, or disputes. Then I'd work with the account manager to have a direct conversation and agree on a payment schedule. I'd ensure our [CRM](https://www.salesforce.com/au/crm/what-is-crm/) has updated AP contact details. If the issue persists, I'd escalate to leadership and discuss revising [credit terms](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/c/credit-terms.asp)."

## Section 7: Process Improvement & Risk Management

### Q15: How do you approach reducing person-dependency risk within a finance team?

**What they're testing:** Operational maturity and team development.

**Model Answer:**
"I believe in three pillars: documentation, cross-training, and delegation. First, I ensure all key processes have documented [SOPs](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/s/standard-operating-procedure.asp) that anyone can follow. Second, I cross-train team members so there's always a backup for every task. Third, where possible, I hand over routine tasks to shared service centres (like an offshore team) while retaining oversight locally. This reduces [key-person risk](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/k/key-person-insurance.asp) significantly."

---

### Q16: Can you give an example of a process improvement you've implemented?

**What they're testing:** Initiative and continuous improvement mindset.

**Model Answer:**
"I identified that our balance sheet reconciliation process was heavily manual and prone to delays. I standardised the templates, introduced a tracking dashboard with deadlines and status updates, and automated some data extraction from the [ERP](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/e/erp.asp). This reduced reconciliation time by about 30% and gave management real-time visibility. It also made the audit process smoother since everything was consistently documented."

---

### Q17: What do you understand by Risk & Control matrices?

**What they're testing:** [Internal controls](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/i/internalcontrols.asp) and governance knowledge.

**Model Answer:**
"A [Risk & Control Matrix (RACM)](https://www2.deloitte.com/content/dam/Deloitte/us/Documents/risk/us-risk-racm-guide.pdf) maps financial processes to the risks inherent in each, and identifies the controls in place — whether they're [preventive or detective](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/i/internalcontrols.asp), manual or automated. I'd work through each process (revenue, AP, payroll), identify what could go wrong, document existing controls, assess their effectiveness, and flag gaps. This aligns with [Group Risk Management](https://www.coso.org/guidance-erm) frameworks and helps prioritise where to strengthen controls."

I use task lists and calendar blocking to stay organised, and I'm transparent with my manager if I need to reprioritise."
